Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What Nexus stores

Your tasks, notes, links, projects and activity history. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. In the optional Android app, notes, voice recordings and screenshots made with the floating bubble are kept in files on your phone until Nexus imports them, and they never leave the device. The bubble only records or captures the screen when you tap Record or Screenshot, and you can stop either at any time.

What leaves your device

Nothing you write is sent to us or to any server. Nexus has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Nexus makes no outside request on its own: its fonts are part of the app. Ask Nexus Find runs on your device. AI answers and file reading are optional and described below. Deleting an item also removes it from your timeline. An optional PIN lock keeps casual snoopers out but does not encrypt your data. Encrypted backups are protected by a passphrase only you know. Links shared into Nexus from other apps are saved on this device.

Your control

Use Settings to export a backup file, restore one, or delete everything. Clearing your browser data also removes your Nexus data, so export a backup first.

Backups and sharing

A normal backup file contains everything you saved, unencrypted. An encrypted backup needs the passphrase you chose. Keep both somewhere safe.

Encryption and sync

If you turn on encryption, what Nexus keeps on this device is encrypted with a key made from your passphrase, which never leaves the device. We cannot reset it. If you turn on sync, Nexus writes encrypted files to a folder you pick or to your own Dropbox, sealed on the device before they leave it. Dropbox (if you use it) sees only encrypted files and file names, and handles them under its own terms. Nexus has no server of its own that sees any of this.

Optional AI providers

If you add AI keys in Settings, Ask Nexus sends your question and the matching notes straight from your browser to the provider that answers (Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras). Nothing goes through our servers, because there are none. Keys are stored only in this browser. Each provider has its own privacy terms, and Google may use free-tier content to improve its products.

Photos, voice notes and files

Attachments are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on this device only. They are included in backup files you export, so those files can be large and are unencrypted. If you tap Read with AI on a photo or PDF, that one file is sent to Anthropic using your own key.

Ask Nexus (optional)

If you choose to use Ask Nexus, you enter your own Anthropic API key. If you set a passphrase, the key is stored encrypted on this device; if you do not, it is kept in memory for the current session only. Delete all data in Settings removes it. When you ask a question, the question and the matching items from your notes are sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. Nothing is sent unless you press Ask.

Android app permissions

The Android app asks only for what each feature needs, and only when you use that feature. Display over other apps draws the floating note bubble, and only while you have switched the bubble on. Microphone records a voice note when you tap Record, and for dictation. Screen capture takes one screenshot when you tap Screenshot, and Android shows its own prompt each time. Calendar (read only) shows your phone calendar beside your tasks on this device, and is never copied or sent anywhere. Notifications and alarms show task reminders. Run at start-up puts reminders and the bubble back after the phone restarts. Internet is used only for the optional features described on this page: sync, AI providers and links you open. Everything you capture stays in the app’s own storage on your phone.
